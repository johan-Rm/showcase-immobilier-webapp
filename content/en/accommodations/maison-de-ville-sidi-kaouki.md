## Guided tour

A seaside house that favours ease and simplicity.

### A freer pace of life a few minutes from the beach

The house fits the spirit of Sidi Kaouki: more open, more relaxed and more outward-facing. The terrace plays an important role in daily use, whether for a morning coffee, coming back from the beach or winding down at the end of the day.

The interior volumes remain warm and easy to make one's own. Three bedrooms allow for family occupancy or repeated stays with friends, without making the property too heavy to manage.

The proximity of the ocean gives the property a genuine destination feel. This is a listing that works well for a secondary residence project or as a holiday home with a more authentic positioning than a classic seaside resort.
