## Guided tour

A seaside house centered on softness and simplicity.

### A freer pace of life a few minutes from the beach

The house reflects the spirit of Sidi Kaouki: more open, more relaxed and more connected to the outdoors. The terrace plays an important role in daily use, whether for morning coffee, returning from the beach or ending the day more quietly.

Interior volumes remain warm and easy to appropriate. Three bedrooms make it possible to imagine family use or repeated stays with friends without turning the property into something heavy to manage.

The proximity to the ocean gives the property a clear destination feel. This listing works especially well for a second-home project or a holiday house with a more authentic positioning than a classic resort product.
