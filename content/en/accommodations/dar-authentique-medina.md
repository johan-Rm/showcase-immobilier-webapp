A medina dar that keeps the charm of traditional architecture while remaining easy to live in.

The heart of the property is organized around the patio, which structures circulation and brings the kind of breathing space buyers look for in medina houses. Light spreads more softly, spaces read more clearly and daily living becomes more comfortable.
