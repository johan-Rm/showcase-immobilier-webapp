## Guided tour

A medina dar that retains the charm of old construction while remaining easy to live in.

### Patio, terrace and the spirit of a true home

The heart of the property is organised around the patio, which structures the circulation and brings that sense of breathing space so sought-after in medina houses. Light diffuses more gently, the volumes read more clearly and daily life gains in comfort.

With three bedrooms, the dar offers a genuine domestic scale. It can accommodate a family, serve as a charming pied-à-terre or support a small short-term rental operation if the project is well framed.

The terrace adds a second level of quality of life. It is a space for rest, meals or relaxation that strengthens the desirability of the property and its capacity to tell a more complete medina experience.
