## Seasonal rental management – MLK My Little Kasbah

Welcome to **MLK – My Little Kasbah**, a local agency in Essaouira specialising in **seasonal rental management**.

Our mission: **to enhance your property**, simplify the rental process and offer your guests an authentic, serene experience inspired by the charm of Essaouira.

---

## Our services

Entrust your property to MLK for **complete and personalised management**:

- Guest welcome on arrival: key handover, property walkthrough and practical advice
- Check-in and check-out inventory, carried out with care to ensure the owner's peace of mind
- Promotion of your property on the **MLK – My Little Kasbah** website, a dedicated showcase for charming rentals in Essaouira
- Booking management, communication and stay follow-up
- Regular maintenance and supervision of the property between each rental

---

## Additional services (paid supplement)

These services are optional and paid directly by guests during their stay:

- **Cleaning**: 120 to 150 MAD per session, depending on the size of the property
- **Home cook**:
  - Breakfast or lunch: from 200 MAD
  - Full dinner (starter, main course, dessert): from 300 MAD

---

## Activities offered by MLK

Essaouira is an iconic destination for wind and ocean enthusiasts.

MLK highlights activities led by trusted local partners:

- **Kitesurfing** and **surfing** lessons (individual or group, all levels)
- Personalised support for an authentic experience
- Selected local activities and excursions

---

## For property owners

By choosing **MLK – My Little Kasbah**, you benefit from tailored support:

- Professional presentation of your property on our website
- Regular monitoring of bookings, payments and guest feedback
- Trusted local management, provided by an available and committed team

---

## Our terms

Our management fees are set at **20% of each rental amount**.

This clear and equitable model ensures a balanced collaboration:

> your income depends on our effectiveness, and vice versa.

---

## Management contract

A transparent contract governs our partnership and specifies:

- A full description of services
- Payment terms and conditions
- Duration and termination conditions
- Management of additional services

## Our philosophy

At **MLK – My Little Kasbah**, we champion character properties while promoting the Essaouira way of life.

Our goal: to ensure the **profitability and peace of mind of property owners**, and to offer guests a memorable stay, between **ocean, authenticity and comfort**.
