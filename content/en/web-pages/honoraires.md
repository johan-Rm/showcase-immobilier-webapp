## Agency fees

The real estate agency **MLK My Little Kasbah**, specialising in property sales in Essaouira and Morocco, applies transparent and balanced fees.

Our agency fees amount to **5% of the sale price of the property**, shared equally between both parties:

- 2.5% payable by the seller
- 2.5% payable by the buyer

This model ensures a fair and balanced relationship between all parties, while providing professional, personalised and secure support at every stage of your real estate project in Essaouira.

With **MLK My Little Kasbah**, you benefit from a reliable service, local expertise and a thorough knowledge of the Moroccan real estate market.
