## Home staging – Showcasing your property in Essaouira

**Home staging** consists of enhancing a property to make it more attractive, without undertaking major works.

In Essaouira, where the experience of the stay plays a key role, a well-presented property allows you to **make an impression from the very first photos**, attract more guests and optimise bookings.

## Why home staging is essential for seasonal rental

In an increasingly competitive market, travellers choose their accommodation in a matter of seconds.

A well-presented property allows you to:

- Increase the **booking rate**
- Improve **positioning on platforms**
- Justify a **higher rental price**
- Create an **immediate wow factor**

The first impression is decisive.

## Simple improvements, real impact

Home staging does not necessarily require major investment.

A few targeted adjustments can transform the appeal of a property:

- Reorganisation of spaces
- Addition of decorative elements (rugs, cushions, lighting)
- Colour and material consistency
- Highlighting natural light

These improvements significantly increase the perception of quality.
